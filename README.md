# Netflix Dataset Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## Project Overview
This project explores and analyzes the Netflix dataset, which consists of movies and TV shows available on the platform. The analysis covers various attributes, such as release year, type, ratings, duration, genres, and more, providing insights into trends, popular content, and other interesting patterns.

---

## Dataset Description
The dataset includes the following columns:
- **show_id**: Unique ID for each show
- **type**: Type of content (Movie/TV Show)
- **title**: Title of the movie or TV show
- **director**: Director of the show
- **cast**: Cast members
- **country**: Country of origin
- **date_added**: Date the content was added to Netflix
- **release_year**: Year the content was released
- **rating**: Content rating (e.g., PG, R, TV-MA)
- **duration**: Duration of the content (in minutes or seasons)
- **listed_in**: Categories/genres of the content
- **description**: Short description of the content

---

## Key Insights
1. **Content Type**:
   - Proportion of movies and TV shows on Netflix shows that movies dominate the platform.

2. **Release Year**:
   - Analysis shows a steady rise in content production, with peaks around certain years.

3. **Duration**:
   - Movies have varying durations, while TV shows are categorized by seasons.

4. **Genres**:
   - The most popular genres include Dramas, Comedies, and International content.

5. **Countries**:
   - The United States leads in content production, followed by India and the UK.

6. **Ratings**:
   - TV-MA and TV-14 are the most common ratings, reflecting the diversity of audience demographics.

7. **Directors and Cast**:
   - Top directors and cast members are highlighted, showing frequent contributors to Netflix's library.
